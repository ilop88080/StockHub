=== CITOU AGENCY TYPOGRAPHY & SPACING RULES ===

1. Font System
   - Titles → Roboto Condensed (Bold or Semi-Bold)
   - Paragraphs → Roboto (Regular)
   - Font scale → 48 / 36 / 28 / 24 / 16 / 14
   - Keep consistent contrast between headings and body

2. Line Height
   - Headings → 1.3
   - Paragraphs → 1.7
   - Keep text airy and readable, never cramped

3. Letter Spacing
   - Titles → +0.5px (optional uppercase)
   - Paragraphs → 0 or slight positive spacing for small sizes

4. Spacing Rhythm
   - Base unit: 8px grid system
   - Small gap → 8px
   - Medium gap → 16px
   - Large gap → 24px
   - Section padding → 80px top & bottom (10 × 8px)
   - Keep vertical spacing consistent between sections

5. Paragraph Width
   - Max width → 70 characters per line
   - Align left, not justified
   - Break text visually with clear margins

6. Button & CTA Text
   - Font: Roboto Condensed, uppercase
   - Weight: 600
   - Letter spacing: +1px
   - Padding: multiple of 8px (ex: 12px 24px)

7. Text Hierarchy
   - H1 → Used once per page, strongest contrast
   - H2 → Section title
   - H3/H4 → Subsection or feature title
   - Body → Supporting content
   - Small → Legal text, captions, or footnotes

8. Visual Balance
   - Maintain consistent rhythm between text blocks
   - Avoid stacking multiple heavy fonts
   - Use white space to breathe around content

9. Color Use
   - Titles: Dark neutral or your brand’s primary tone (#005522)
   - Highlights / Accents: #217844
   - Body text: Dark gray or neutral (#222)

10. Responsiveness
    - Use fluid typography where possible (e.g., clamp)
    - Always respect the 8px spacing rule on all breakpoints
